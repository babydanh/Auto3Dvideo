# Example Brief — 3D Tournament Product Explainer

## Audience

A small sports organization or tournament organizer evaluating a digital tournament-management product.

## Objective

Explain how a tournament organizer creates a competition, manages a bracket and follows live scores in a 30-second vertical video.

## Message

The product turns tournament setup and live match tracking into one clear workflow.

## Tone

Confident, friendly, technical but understandable. Avoid unverified performance claims and avoid showing real personal data.

## Deliverables

- One 30-second 9:16 social video.
- One 16:9 master variant.
- Vietnamese narration with English subtitle fixture.
- Thumbnail and delivery manifest.

## Visual direction

Use a polished stylized 3D dashboard, miniature stadium environment, navy/electric-blue palette and warm orange accent. UI text must be added in a deterministic editor layer rather than generated by the video model.

## Safety and rights

Use fictional product data, self-created or approved assets, no real athlete likeness and no unlicensed music or logos. The example is a fixture and is not an advertisement or performance certification.
