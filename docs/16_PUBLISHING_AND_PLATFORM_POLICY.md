# Publishing and Platform Policy Workflow

## Scope

Auto3Dvideo prepares platform-specific files and evidence. It does not assume that a generated video is monetizable or approved for publication. Automatic publishing is disabled in MVP.

## Platform preparation

| Platform | Preparation |
|---|---|
| TikTok | Vertical variant, captions, thumbnail/cover, commercial disclosure state and AI-label decision |
| Douyin | China-market account/commerce eligibility, Mandarin/localization, rights and platform-specific account review |
| YouTube | Master/short variants, captions, thumbnail, altered/synthetic-content decision and monetization-policy review |
| Instagram/Reels | Vertical variant, caption, music/rights and account-specific branded-content state |
| Client delivery | Contract scope, brand approval, final files and usage rights |

## Publish gate

The publish job is `BLOCKED` until the delivery is technically validated, rights are approved, AI disclosure is resolved, platform profile is current, the human reviewer approves and the user explicitly confirms the external action.

## Reused content

The workflow rejects a plan that only downloads, translates, crops, revoices or watermarks another creator's video without documented permission and meaningful original contribution. Authorized localization requires a written scope for derivative use, platforms, territories and monetization.

## AI disclosure

The project records whether the video includes realistic or meaningfully altered synthetic media and what disclosure is required by the target platform. Disclosure is not treated as a license and does not guarantee monetization.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not automate account/region bypass, unauthorized scraping, watermark removal, fake engagement, impersonation, copyright evasion, spam uploads or a publish action based only on a generated file.
